# House style for edits

## Pacing

- No pause longer than 0.15 seconds unless it is there on purpose.
- The hook (first 3 seconds) is never cut into. Lead with the swing, the lift, or the result line.
- When a line is repeated, the last full take wins.
- Keep reels 15 to 40 seconds. Shorter wins when the point is one cue.

## Captions

- Two lines max, 1 to 3 words per line, one emphasised word per line.
- No emojis in captions.
- Keep captions in the lower-middle safe zone; never cover the club head or the barbell path.

## Look

- Clean and natural. Light auto colour and white balance only, no heavy grade.
- No logo or lower-third for now.

## Sound

- Loudness normalised to -14 LUFS.
- Keep natural sound (club strike, plates) audible under any music.
- Music bed allowed on session montages, ducked under speech. No music on pure instruction reels.
- Sound effects: none, except a subtle hit on the before/after switch.

## Never do

- No zoom punches on every sentence.
- No stock footage.
- No slow-motion longer than 2 seconds on a swing unless the reel is a swing teardown.
- Never cut mid-swing or mid-rep.

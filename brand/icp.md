# Who we talk to

## The person

Recreational golfers (roughly 10 to 25 handicap) and people who train in the gym 3 to 5 times a week, mostly 20s to 40s. They watch short-form content for tips they can try at their next session.

## What they want

- Hit the ball more consistently and further without a full swing rebuild.
- Get stronger and more mobile, and see it carry over to their sport.
- Feel like someone relatable is on the same journey.

## What keeps them stuck

- Too many swing tips, no idea which one to trust.
- Gym programs that are not built around their sport or their time.
- No feedback loop: they never film themselves.

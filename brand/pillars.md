# Pillars, angles and stories

## Main angle

Getting better at golf and in the gym is the same skill: show up, track it, change one thing at a time.

## Sub-angles

- One cue at a time beats ten swing thoughts.
- Strength and mobility work are golf practice.
- Filming yourself is the fastest feedback loop.
- Consistency over intensity: boring sessions add up.
- Misses are data. Show the bad reps too.
- Short practice with a plan beats long practice without one.

## Story structures

1. **Before and after**: the swing or lift last month, what changed, how it looks now.
2. **The mistake**: a fault I had for months, what it cost, the fix.
3. **The contrarian call**: the common tip, why it didn't work for me, what did.
4. **The teardown**: one swing or one set, frame by frame.
5. **The list with a twist**: three cues, and the third is the surprise.
6. **The session**: one real workout or range session, compressed, one lesson.
7. **The question I get most**: the question, the usual answer, my answer.

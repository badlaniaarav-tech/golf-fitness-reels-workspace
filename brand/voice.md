# How we sound

## In one line

A friend who is actually putting in the work, showing what they did today and the one thing they learned. Not a guru.

## How we talk

- Short sentences. First person. Present tense where possible ("here's what I changed").
- Open with the result or the problem in one line, never with "hey guys" or "in today's video".
- Plain words. Explain any golf or lifting jargon in half a sentence the first time.
- Honest about misses: a bad swing or a failed rep is content, not something to hide.
- Close with one clear takeaway, then the call to action (follow for the next session).
